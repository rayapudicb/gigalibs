## Implemented

- Migration `000002_partner_financial_extensions.up.sql` adds a Party and role projection, customer/vendor account overlays, vault-token-only bank references, append-only credit deltas, holds, inbox/outcome tables and a local organization replica. Every business table has forced tenant RLS; the migration runner denies runtime updates/deletes on the credit ledger.
- Kernel organization activation queues this module through the existing `module.call` page/complete protocol. Kernel Party/role outbox facts are delivered through a body-bound Ed25519 relay token to the module's internal inbox. The inbox stores a receipt with the projection in one tenant transaction and refuses missing prior versions for retry.
- Customer/vendor account create/list routes require signed user context, an explicit active owning unit, an active canonical Party role and a signed live Finance check for an active, class-matching subledger control account. The Finance check returns the account/book/version; account creation, audit and outbox are one local transaction. Exact credit exposure arithmetic is in a pure function.
- Two credential generator scripts write Ed25519 pairs only to ignored local compose env files; values are not printed. No credential was generated in this worktree.
- AR migration 17 adds organization ownership and a unique fulfillment source key to invoices, preserving old NULL owners until reconciliation. Database triggers reject new ownerless documents and owner changes. AR stores a local forced-RLS organization replica delivered by the kernel job; its sync route accepts signed platform context only, with no direct OIDC fallback. Invoice create/update/issue/get/list checks the signed organization version and owning unit; its metadata window now includes the owner field.
- Orders migration 5 leases only dispatched fulfillment facts. The hosted Orders relay signs the exact envelope for AR's private inbox; AR no longer accepts fulfillment bodies on its user invoice route. The AR inbox checks token source, audience, tenant, message and raw body. Fulfillment invoice creation fails closed without a mapped Party/customer and configured billing entity/series; successful issue writes invoice, audit, outbox and replay receipt in one AR transaction. A third credential generator script prepares this relay pair without printing secrets.
- AR migration 18 adds a tenant-RLS credit source position and dedicated outbox. Invoice issue, payment (including credit-note settlement) and void inserts queue ordered open-balance snapshots in the same transaction. The AR relay signs each fact for the Business Partner credit inbox, which enforces Party projection, owner/currency stability, exact source version order, stale-version rejection and unchanged body on duplicate message IDs. It appends balance differences to the immutable credit ledger. Historical invoices without an owner or canonical Party remain outside the feed pending explicit reconciliation; credit approvals remain unimplemented and must stay closed.

## Migration compatibility and roll-forward

Business Partner migrations 2–3 are additive to an undeployed generated module schema. They do not copy the Party master or touch customer data. AR migration 17 adds nullable owner columns so old rows survive; new inserts are owner-required and existing non-NULL owners are immutable. AR migration 18 adds tables/triggers without changing existing invoices; it does not backfill old ownerless or unmapped documents. Orders migration 5 adds a filtered lease function without changing old outbox rows. A failed credit delivery remains in the source outbox for ordered retry or dead-letter inspection; later versions cannot overtake it. Correct a bad projection with a forward migration or corrected higher-version fact, then reconcile the final AR balance with the Business Partner source position. The isolated proof runs against containers created and removed by the tests, not UAT.
