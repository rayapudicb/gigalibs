- **Size:** L

## Design

- **Module:** `safety-operations`, generated by `pnpm module:new` in the module host; readiness PLANNED.
- **Data:** `hazard_reports` (operation id per reporter, severity GOOD_CATCH / UNSAFE_CONDITION / STOP_WORK, category,
  description, area, optional site and asset references, observed time, triage due by severity: 48 h, 8 h,
  immediate; REPORTED → TRIAGED → CLOSED; risk likelihood × consequence 1–5 and a route: MAINTENANCE, TRAINING,
  INVESTIGATION, RESOLVED); `hazard_photos` (append-only, ≤ 300 KB, sniffed); `stop_work_alerts` (one per STOP_WORK
  report; OPEN → LIFTED with a reason, by someone other than the reporter) and `stop_work_acknowledgements`
  (append-only, one per person); `hazard_investigations` (5-Why, root cause, contributing factors, completion).
- **Permissions:** `safety-operations.hazards.report|read|triage`, `safety-operations.stop-work.acknowledge|lift`,
  `safety-operations.investigations.manage`. Roles: Frontline worker (report), Safety champion (report, read, triage,
  acknowledge, investigations), EHS director (all).
- **Web:** `/safety-operations` — open stop-work banner, triage board, report detail (photo, rating, route, close),
  investigations, area and category counts, CSV export.
- **Mobile:** `/mobile/v1/safety/...` (report with photo, my reports, open stop-work alerts, acknowledge); feature
  `safety`.

## Gates

ADR-0036 review; CMMS remediation command contract; push/audible stop-work broadcast; statutory logs (OSHA/RIDDOR)
with redaction; voice notes and photo markup; location beacons; offline capture; device proof; owner UAT.
